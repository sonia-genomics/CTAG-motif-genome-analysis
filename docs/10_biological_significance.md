# BIOLOGICAL SIGNIFICANCE OF CTAG SUPPRESSION

CTAG suppression represents a major example of genomic
sequence heterogeneity in bacteria.

The suppression likely reflects combined effects of:

- Mutation bias
- Repair mechanisms
- Codon selection
- Structural constraints
- Evolutionary selection

The association of CTAG hotspots with genomic heterogeneity
suggests possible biological roles in:

- Genome organization
- Replication
- Regulation
- Adaptation

Understanding CTAG suppression may provide broader insights into:

- Bacterial evolution
- Genome architecture
- Sequence selection mechanisms
- Regulatory genomics
