# CHAPTER 5 — RESULTS

Analysis of bacterial genomes demonstrated strong
under-representation of CTAG motifs.

---

# Major Findings

## 1. Global CTAG Suppression

CTAG showed O/E < 1 in all analyzed bacterial genomes.

In most genomes:

- CTAG ranked among the rarest tetranucleotides.

---

## 2. TAG Stop Codon Suppression

TAG was the least abundant termination codon compared to:

- TAA
- TGA

This pattern persisted across:

- Whole genomes
- Coding regions
- Termination sites

---

## 3. Cytosine Effect

Adding a 5′ cytosine before TAG further increased suppression.

CTAG was significantly more suppressed than:

- ATAG
- GTAG
- TTAG

---

## 4. Local CTAG Clusters

Despite global suppression, CTAG formed local high-density clusters.

These clusters exceeded expectations from random Poisson distribution.

---

## 5. Base Heterogeneity Association

CTAG hotspots frequently overlapped with regions of:

- High base heterogeneity
- Uneven nucleotide composition

---

## 6. Entropy Analysis

Low-entropy genomic regions often showed elevated CTAG clustering,
suggesting non-random structural organization.

---

## 7. Functional Enrichment

Hotspot-associated genes showed enrichment in:

- Replication
- DNA repair
- Stress response
- Regulatory pathways
